
## 1. Project Overview

Mental health has become an increasingly important concern among university students due to academic pressure, lifestyle changes, social expectations, and personal challenges. Educational institutions collect survey data to better understand the well-being of their students, but raw survey responses alone do not provide meaningful insights.

This project aims to analyze student mental health survey data and transform it into actionable insights using data analytics techniques. The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow, including data understanding, cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL-based querying, and interactive dashboard development. The final outcome is a Power BI dashboard that enables stakeholders to explore mental health trends, identify key factors affecting student well-being, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 2. Business Problem

The university has collected survey responses from students to understand their mental health and well-being. However, the survey data exists as raw records, making it difficult for administrators to identify patterns, monitor trends, or make informed decisions.

The objective of this project is to analyze the survey data, uncover meaningful relationships between demographic, academic, lifestyle, and mental health factors, and present the findings through an interactive dashboard that supports evidence-based decision-making.

## 3. Stakeholders

The primary stakeholders for this project include:

* University Administration
* Student Affairs Department
* Academic Counselors
* Mental Health and Wellness Teams
* Researchers interested in student well-being

## 4. Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze the demographic profile of survey participants.
* Measure the prevalence of stress, anxiety, depression, and isolation among students.
* Explore relationships between academic performance, workload, sleep patterns, and mental health indicators.
* Identify lifestyle factors associated with student well-being.
* Build an interactive dashboard for exploring survey results.
* Provide data-driven insights and recommendations to support student wellness initiatives.

## 5. Project Scope

This project focuses on the analysis of a student mental health survey dataset. The scope includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL querying, statistical summaries, and interactive dashboard development using Power BI.

The project does not attempt to diagnose mental health conditions or establish causal relationships. The findings are limited to the information available in the survey dataset.

## 6. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it:

* Produces a clean and reliable dataset for analysis.
* Answers key business questions using data.
* Presents findings through clear and interactive visualizations.
* Identifies meaningful trends and patterns within the survey data.
* Delivers practical recommendations based on the analysis.
* Demonstrates a complete end-to-end data analytics workflow suitable for a professional portfolio.
